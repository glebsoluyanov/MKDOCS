## Changes to Existing Quests

- The second class transfer quest reward for all races excluding the Kamael (Dark Elf, Dwarf, Elf, Human, and Orc) has been increased.
- The soul crystal stage that is possible through the quest Enhance Your Weapon has been increased to stage 15. You can absorb the soul from the boss monsters Beleth, Antharas, and Valakas.
- Isle of Prayer - While obtaining a stage 14 soul crystal from the raid boss Baylor in the Crystal Caverns, you can now attack Baylor with the "Good Crystal Golems" and still obtain the soul.
- The level range of the Repent Your Sins quest has been increased to level 85.
- The reward for completing the Arrogant Search quest has been changed for the Kamael. (All other races will receive the same item reward according to their class.)
