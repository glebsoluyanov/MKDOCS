# RESURRECTION & SCROLL OF ESCAPE DESTINATIONS

When you **Return to Village** after dying, or cast a Scroll of Escape, you reappear in the nearest town. But which town is that?

Heavy lines on the maps mark the territory for each “Return/Escape” town; the towns themselves are marked with a black X. High-karma characters arrive near town, not in it.

Make it a habit to know where you’d escape an Escape. A well timed Escape might save you several minutes’ travel; a poorly timed Escape might drop you in an even worse predicament than you escaped from.

---

> Note that a Return from the southern seas sends you to Giran Harbor.
> 
> Also remember that Elves and Dark Elves return to their own home town, rather than to the enemy’s village, and that high-karma characters near Dion return to Floran instead.
