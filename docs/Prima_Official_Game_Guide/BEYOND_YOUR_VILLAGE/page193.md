## 🏰 Key Features

- **Central Hub**: Large cross-shaped corridor connecting all wings.
- **Stairs to Level 2**: Located at top-center, marked with an X icon.
- **North Direction**: Clearly labeled on map.
- **Room Density**: Higher concentration of elite units (marked with *) in rooms 1–9 and 18–22.
- **Premio Class Only in Rooms 14–22**: Introduced as higher-tier combatants vs. Excurio/Krator/Porta/Snipe/Mordeo/Ricenseo.

## ⚔️ Tactical Notes

- Elite units (* Krator, Snipe, etc.) appear frequently — likely for high-level encounters or boss areas.
- Premio class appears only in rooms 14–22 — possibly representing dungeon elites or guardians.
- Porta and Ricenseo provide support roles (healer/buff?).
- Cohorts act as lieutenants under main units.
