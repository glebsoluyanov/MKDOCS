# LINEAGE II
## PRIMA Official Game Guide

### CRUMA TOWER, FLOOR 2 & FLOOR 3

---

#### FLOOR 2 Map Overview:
- **To Level 1** (at the top)
- **North** is indicated on the map.
- Maps courtesy of Duncan.

#### Key Locations on Floor 2:
1. **Premo, Torfe, Validus, Dark Lord** - Various monsters found in different areas marked with circles numbered ① to ⑫.

#### FLOOR 3 Map Overview:
- **CORE** room at the top of the map.
- **Locked door** leading back to Level 1.
- **From Level 2**: Exit teleporters appear once you kill Core (Bring 2 keys).
- **Fall to Level 2**.

#### Key Locations on Floor 3:
1. **Torfe, Validus, Dark Lord, Death Knight** - Monsters marked with circles numbered ① to ⑤.
