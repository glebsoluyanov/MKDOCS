---|---|---
Trader Payne is famous for selling hand-made arrowheads, but he has recently been requested to create a hooked one, made of animal claws. He asks for help, and needs fifty wolf claws. |

---|---|---
The Queen of the Pixies is preparing a spell to protect birds that are nesting in the Elven Forest from keltirs and wolves. Pixy Murika is under the Queen's order to collect the needed ingredients. She gives you one sparkling red Keltir or Gray Wolf Fangs. In return she gives you a spark stone that was found in the forest. |

2. Hunt Red Keltirs and Gray Wolves until you gather $100$ Predator's Fangs. Many wolves are close to Murika. |
3. Give the fangs to Pixy Murika, for a Shard of Glass ($100a$), Onyx ($300a$) or an Emerald ($5000a$). |

**SHILEN'S HUNT** | **LEVEL 3** | **DARK ELVEN VILLAGE**

A ceremony for Shilen and Gran Kain will be held soon in the Dark Elven Village. Before the ceremony there is a hunt for wild animals for offering, called Shilen's Hunt. If you can hunt Brown Keltirs and Ashen Wolves during Shilen's Hunt, you will earn Dark Bezooars. Collect thirteen and take them to Sentry Nelsya. 3. Return to Sentry Nelsya with the Bezooars, for a Lesser Healing Potion and $1900\text{ EXP}$. |

**PROOF OF VALOR** | **LEVEL 4** | **ORC VILLAGE**
---|---|---
Praetorian Rukain always treats young Orcs like little kids. He says that those who desire to be acknowledged as Orc Fighters must prove their valor by hunting Kasha Wolves and bringing back fifty of their Fangs as proof. |

**REVENGE OF THE REDBONNET** | **LEVEL 4** | **DWARVEN VILLAGE**
---|---|---
Maryse Redbonnet lost her family as a child and nearly lost her own life to wolves, and so she burns with hatred for them. Grant her wish by hunting Black Wolves around the village, then gather forty of their pelts and take them back to Maryse to receive her grandmother's heirloom. |
1. Talk to Maryse Redbonnet $\text{K}$. |
2. Hunt Black Wolves until you have $40$ Black Wolf Pelts. You will find plenty north of the path. |
3. Deliver the $40$ pelts to Maryse. |
