### Right Column:

#### TRIAL OF THE GUILDSMAN
**Artisan; Scavenger**

1. Talk to Warehouse Keeper Valkon (Giran W). To earn respect in the Dwarven society as an official guildsman you must pass the Trial of the Guildsman by creating seven Journeyman’s Rings. To register for the quest you must give Valkon 2000 adena.

2. Valkon tells you to talk to Blacksmith Altran (Talking Island D), but because he is quite irritable, Valkon suggests you take him a Mandragora Berry. (Do so.)

3. Mandragora Berries are dropped by Mandragora Blossoms and Saplings in the west side of the Execution Grounds. Get a berry.

4. Talk to Altran. He gives you a recipe for a Journeyman’s Ring:
   - 1 Journeyman Gem (unique quest item)
   - 1 Journeyman Deco Bead (unique quest item)
   - 1 Steel
   - 10 Varnish
   - 10 D-Grade Crystals

He also gives you two contacts to help you create the rings.

5. Get the common items either through hunting or trade. Grand total for seven rings: 7 steel, 70 varnish, 70 D-Grade Crystals.

6. To get the Journeyman Gem, talk to Warehouse Keeper Norman (Gludin W). He sends you to Hunters' Village to talk to Blacksmith Duning B.

7. Duning tells you to hunt Breka Orcs and get his 30 Keys back. To find Breka Orcs, head north out of Giran. At the end of the path you will find various types of Breka Orcs.

8. Once you have 30 Keys, return to Duning.

9. Daning sends you back to Norman. Norman gives you a list of items you will need to make the Gem. Fortunately, each creature in this part of the trial drops 2 items.

10. 70 Granite Whetstones. Go to the Wastelands and kill Granite Golems.

11. 70 Gray Bone Powder. Get this from Ghoul or Straigns in the west side of the Execution Grounds.

12. 70 Red Pigment. Hunt Dead Seekers, also in the Execution Grounds.

13. 70 Braided Yarn. Hunt Silenos, around the path north of Giran.

14. Go back to Norman. He gives you 7 Journeyman Gems.

#### TRIAL OF THE PILGRIM
**CLERIC; ELVEN & SHILLIEN ORACLES; ORC SHAMAN**

1. Talk to Hermit Santiago. He is on a farm with Piotur, north of the Orc Barracks. He says those who wish to practice divine powers must understand their nature through the Trial of the Pilgrim. The myths that have been passed down are all false, and you must learn about the gods from the very beginning.

2. Talk to Seer Tanapi (Orc Village A).

3. Head deep into the Cave of Trials (through Room #11, into a recently opened chamber), and talk to Ancestor Martankus, whose soul currently rests in a large stone head.

4. Martankus sends you to kill Lava Salamander in the Forgotten Temple until you obtain the Essence of Flame.

5. Once you have the flame, return to Ancestor Martankus and talk to him, then return to Seer Tanapi.

6. Travel to the Dwarven Village and talk to Gauri Twinklerock B.

7. Talk to Priest of Earth Gerald B. You must give him 100,000 adena.

8. Talk to Wanderer Dorff N.

9. Return to Priest of Earth Gerald to be refunded your 100,000 adena.

10. Talk to Priest Prim (Giran B).

11. Travel to Talking Island and talk to Priest Petron A.

12. Hunt Nahir in the Elven Fortress on Talking Island; once you get the Hair of Nahir, return to Petron.

13. Talk to Andellia in the Elven Village C. She sends you to the Waterfall between the Elf and Dark Elf areas to kill Black Willows.

14. After you get the Debris of Willow, talk to Uruha; she is right by the Black Willows.

15. Talk to Andellia again. She tells you to burn the Book of Darkness.

16. Talk to Wiseman Casian. He can be found southwest of the south Ant Nest entrance (south of the “a” in “Wasteland” on your ingame map).

17. Return to Hermit Santiago to receive the Mark of Pilgrim.

#### TRIAL OF THE SCHOLAR
**WIZARDS: HUMAN, ELVEN, DARK**

1. Talk to Magister Mirien (Dion A). She explains that in order to pass the Trial of the Scholar you must complete errands given by each of the highest mages. The first person to meet is High Priest Sylvain of the same temple.

2. Talk to Sylvain, then talk to Maria M. Maria sends you to Captain Lucas 1.

3. After you talk to Captain Lucas return to Maria. She sends you to talk to Astrologer Creta (Floran C).

4. Ask Creta about the thief, then return to Maria.

5. Talk to Maria and get the handbag from her. Return to Creta.

6. Creta draws a picture for you. Take the picture to Maria.
