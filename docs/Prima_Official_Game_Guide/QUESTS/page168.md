# SECOND QUESTS (LEVEL 37 TESTIMONIES)

## **Testimony of Trust**  
**All Humans**

1. Talk to High Priest Hollint (Oren B). He says that for the Human race to survive in the Chaotic Chronicle, they must earn the trust of the other races and combine forces with them. The Testimony of Trust is for you to become an envoy of Humans, and meet the leaders of each race in the name of Trust.

2. Talk to Tetrarch Asterios (Elven Village A). He sends you to find Leul of Zephyr Winds and Aktea of Verdant Wild.

3. Head west from 4 towards the waterfall. When you kill a Dryad, Aktea of Verdant Wild will appear. Kill her.

4. Continue west until you find a Lirein. Kill the Lirein and Leul of Zephyr Winds will appear. Kill her and return to Asterios.

5. Talk to Tetrarch Thifiell (Dark Elven Village A). He sends you to Magister Clayton (Dion D).

6. Magister Clayton sends you to kill 10 Guardian Basilisks in the Dark Pass; when you collect 10 bottles of blood, you get Basilisk Plasma.

7. Go to the Wasteland and kill 10 Ant Soldiers. After you collect 10 Giant Aphids you will get Honey Dew.

8. Finally, go to Cruma Swamp and kill 10 Marsh Stakatos. Once you have collected 10 Stakatos fluids you will get Stakato Ichor. Now return to Hollint.

9. Talk to Thifiell. He tells you that Talin’s Sword has been stolen.

10. Kill Marsh Spiders in Cruma Swamp (right outside the tower) for 20 Spider Ichor.

11. Kill Harpies, just north of Giran Castle, for 20 Harpy’s Down.

12. Talk to Adonius. Get the book, then talk to Arkenia again.

13. Talk to Thalia. She tells you that Talin’s Sword has been stolen.

14. Talk to Israel Silvershadow (Hunters’ Village H). She needs materials.

15. Kill Leto Lizardmen, all around Oren. After you get the 4 gems, the shaft, and the blade, return to Silvershadow.

16. Israel reassembles the spear.

17. Talk to Thalia. She gives you the Grail of Purity and sends you out to kill a Unicorn of Eva.

18. The Unicorns are all over the southern half of the Elven Forest; you must kill one and get the last hit with the spear.

19. After you get the Tears of Unicorn, return once more to Thalia. She gives you the Water of Life.

20. Talk to Tetrarch Asterios.

21. Now return to Master Cardien to get the Mark of Life.

## **Testimony of Fate**  
**All Dark Elves**

1. Talk to Magister Kaira (Giran F). She tells you that as you find more hidden knowledge, your power will increase those who uncover the truth, your knowledge will gain power. Those who uncover the truth, your knowledge will gain power. Rewarded with the Mark of Fate to honor the deed of increasing spiritual heritage. She sends you to Brother Metheus O.

2. Metheus sends you to the Execution Ground (near the giant guillotine) to kill a Hangman Tree. Return to Metheus.

3. Talk to Master Ixia (Dion D). She needs materials.

4. Kill Tyrants (the middle of the Wastelands) to get 10 Tyrant’s Bloods.

5. Kill Marsh Stakatos and Marsh Spiders (Crumba Swamp) to get 10 Nightshade Roots and 10 Marsh Spider Fluids.

6. Kill Dead Seekers (Execution Ground) to get 10 Dead Seekers Drugs.

7. Kill Medusas (east from Giran) to get 10 Medusa Ichor. Return to Ixia.

8. Talk to Metheus. He gives you the Elder’s Skull to give to Kaira.

9. Talk to Kaira. She conjures Adler’s Spirit. Talk to Adler, who sends you to Magister Roa.

10. Talk to Magister Roa A. She gives you a receipt.

11. Talk to Warehouse Keeper Norman (Gludin W). Now return to Kaira.

To continue, you must be Level 38.

12. Kaira sends you to Tetrarch Thifiell (Dark Elven Village A).

13. Thifiell gives you to Arkenia at the Altar of Rites. Arkenia tells you to get a special kind of ink.

14. Talk to a Bloody Pixy and a Blight Treant. They are common all over the Dark Elven land.

15. Now go to the swamp west of the waterfall. Kill a Black Willow Lurker, in the swamp west of the waterfall between the Elven and Dark Elven Villages.

16. Hunt Breka Orc Overlords (exit Giran 4 and travel north).

17. Hunt Grandis (10). Head up Death Pass, but don’t cross the bridge into Oren territory. Instead, turn east and run along the north rim of the Valley of Dragons. After awhile you will run into Grandis.

18. Hunt Karul Bugbears south of the east exit of the tunnel that leads to Hunters’ Village.
