# FIRST PROFESSION QUESTS

## PATH TO A WARRIOR - LEVEL 19 GLUDIN

1. Talk to Master Auron [A] and get Auron’s (Aymus’s) Letter.
2. Take the letter to Trader Simplon (Gludio X). He gives you a Warrior Guild Mark.
3. Go to the Ruins of Sorrow (Ruins of Agony). Hunt Tracker Skeletons and Tracker Skeleton Leaders northwest of the walled ruins area until you collect 10 Rusted Bronze Swords.
4. Talk to Trader Simplon. He collects the mark and gives you a Rusted Bronze Sword and Simplon’s Letter.
5. Take the letter to Auron. He turns the sword into a usable item.
6. Hunt Poison Spiders and Arachnid Trackers southeast of Gludio along the path until you collect 20 Poison Spider’s Legs. You *must* use only the Rusted Bronze Sword to kill the spiders, so take lots of Soulshots (because the sword is very weak) and a pile of Antidotes for the poison.
7. Give the legs to Auron, and get a Medallion Of Warrior.
8. When you are Level 20, talk to Grand Master Ramos (Gludio [A]) to become a Warrior.

## PATH TO A HUMAN KNIGHT - LEVEL 19 GLUDIN

1. Talk to Sir Klaus Vasper [O]. He requests 3 Coins of Lords.
2. Talk to High Priestess Levian [B]. She requests 20 Totems from Langk Lizardmen.
3. Talk to Captain Bezique [I]. He requests 20 Poison Spider Legs from Poison Spiders.
4. Talk to both Levian and Bezique and do their quests at the same time because you can find both types of creatures just south of Gludio (on the west side of the path).
5. Take 20 Poison Spider Legs to Bezique and 20 Langk Lizardmen Totems to Levian to receive 2 Coins of Lords.
6. Talk to Captain Bathis (Gludio [2]). He requests 10 Bugbear Necklaces from Bugbear Raiders, northeast of Gludio (on the east side of the path, in the small set of ruins).
7. Take 10 Bugbear Necklaces to Bathis for third Coin of Lords.
8. Talk to Vaspe. He gives you a Sword of Ritual.
9. When you are Level 20, take the sword to Grand Master Ramos. He grants you Knighthood.

## PATH TO A ROGUE - LEVEL 19 GLUDIN

1. Talk to Captain Bezique [I]. He gives you Bezique’s Letter.
2. Take the letter to Neti [N]. She gives you Neti’s Dagger and Bow. You can *only* use these two weapons during the entire quest, so take lots of Soulshots — they’re weak against Undead.
3. Go to the Ruins of Sorrow (Ruins of Agony). Hunt Spartoi (with the dagger or bow) anywhere in this area or to the northwest of the walled ruins until you collect 10 Spartoi’s Bones. You can also hunt Skeletons, but collect rarely Spartoi’s Bones.
4. Give the bones to Neti and you get a Horseshoe Of Light.
5. Give the horseshoe to Bezique and he gives you a Wanted Bill.
6. Hunt Cat’s Eye Bandits (with the dagger or bow) until you collect the 4 stolen items — Stolen Necklace, Stolen Tomes, Stolen Jewelry and Stolen Ring. They are along south side of the road from Gludio to Gludio (near -49K, 147K and north). Take some friends to deal with all the other OI Mahums (up to Level 30+), or just be very careful to find the bandits alone.
7. Talk to Captain Bezique. He takes all the quest-related items and gives you Bezique’s Recommendation.
8. When you are Level 20, talk to Grand Master Ramos (Gludio [A]) to become a Rogue.

## PATH TO A HUMAN WIZARD - LEVEL 19 GLUDIN

1. Talk to Parina [B]. To become a Human Wizard, you must have the four Tokens of Water, Fire, Wind and Earth.
2. **Token of Fire.** Talk to the Fire Salamander near the Ruins of Despair, south of Gludio.
3. Kill Pinrul spiders north of the Ruins until you get the Key of Flame. Return to Salamander to get a Token of Fire.
4. **Token of Wind.** Find the Wind Sylph. She is at the northwest entrance of the Wastelands beyond the southwestern mountains.
5. Speak with the Lizardman of Wasteland in the southwest Wastelands.
6. Return to Sylph and collect a Token of Wind.
7. **Token of Water.** Speak with Water Undine, near Permel Lake, a little further north past the Abandoned Camp in the north.
8. Kill the Water Seer and bring back two Sparkle Pebbles to get a Token of Water from Undine.
9. **Token of Earth.** Find the Earth Snake, just north of the Ruins of Agony.
10. Hunt Red Bear around the ruins until you get Red Soil.
11. Return to the Earth Snake for a Token of Earth.
12. Go back to Parina and get the Bead of Four Seasons.
13. When you are Level 20, talk to High Priestess Levian (Gludin [B]) to become a Wizard.

## PATH TO A CLERIC - LEVEL 19 GLUDIN

1. Talk to Priest Zigaunt [B]. You must prove your pure faith by removing all inappropriate books that criticize the current order. The first rumors about those books appeared in Gludio. Go and investigate the book, *Understanding of Miracles*. He gives you a Letter of Order.
2. Go to Gludio and talk to Trader Simplon X.
3. Talk to Guard Praga [3] and the other by Priestess Vivyan C about the other two books.
4. Kill Ruin Zombies just outside Ruins of Sorrow/Agony to get Pendant.
5. Return to Praga for book.
6. Return to Priest Zigaunt.
7. Find Lionel on Talking Island, near the road exiting the Elven Ruins, and deliver letter.
8. Take book to Grand Magister Gallint [I].
9. Return to Lionel and get the Covenant.
10. When you are Level 20, return to Gludin and Speak with Priest Zigaunt to become a Cleric.

## PATH TO AN ELVEN KNIGHT - LEVEL 19 GLUDIO

1. Tell Master Sorius [A] that you wish to become an Elven Knight. He sends you to the Ruins of Sorrows (Ruins of Agony), to defeat and collect the 20 Topaz Pieces.
2. Take the topaz to Sorius. He gives you a letter and sends you to get an Elven Knight Brooch from Kluto.
3. Take the letter to Blacksmith Kluto (Gludin S). He requests that you thin out the OI Mahum Novices at the Abandoned Camp, and return with 20 Emerald Pieces as proof.
4. Hunt Novices and return to Kluto. He exchanges the Topaz and Emerald Pieces for a completed Elven Knight Brooch.
5. Return to Sorius. He passes you on to Grand Master Rains.
6. When you are Level 20, talk to Grand Master Rains (Gludio [A]) to become an Elven Knight.
