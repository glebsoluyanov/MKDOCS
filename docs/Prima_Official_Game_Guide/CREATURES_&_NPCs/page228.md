# NPCs

This last list of non-player characters lists all the people who (in the normal course of events), you won’t have reason to fight. It includes trainers, traders, town guards and all the other people you meet and converse with. Many are part of quests that you will undertake.

For each NPC, we list his race, general role in life, and the zone(s) where you can find him.

Several of the guards are archers. In those cases, “archer; x2; rf” means that he has twice the normal range, and that he has rapid fire.
