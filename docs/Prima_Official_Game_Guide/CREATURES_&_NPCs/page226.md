# SIEGE DEFENDERS

These are the NPC troops defending a castle under siege. They are the same for Dion, Giran, Gludio and Oren (with the exception of their commanders); they are a bit stronger for Aden.

Siege defenders cannot be Held, Paralyzed, Confused or Slept. They are up to 50% faster than other mobs in the game.

All stats match the Creature stats (explained on p. 201).
